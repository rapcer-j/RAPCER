DIGITAL HELP TERMS & CONDITIONS

Review the RAPCER Digital Help process before submitting your request.

How the Process Works

  • Select the services you need.
  • Explain your request in a few words.
  • RAPCER reviews the request and contacts you by email.
  • Pricing and instructions are confirmed privately.
  • A private payment link is sent only when RAPCER confirms it is ready.

Available Assistance

  • Job applications and résumés
  • PDFs and document organization
  • Email, accounts, apps and devices
  • Google Business, QR codes and basic business setup
  • General online forms and registrations

Important

Do not send payment before RAPCER reviews the request. Digital Help provides technology and administrative assistance only.
